This is Android 13+ only, shown once, when the first big download starts (voice, translation or scan pack) and only if notifications aren’t allowed yet. It never shows on app launch. Continue opens Android’s own permission prompt. Not now skips it. Either way, the download starts at once and still runs when the phone is locked. Android just hides the notification. iOS doesn’t show this screen, since a background download needs no permission there. The size in the copy is the pack’s real size. The copy is a draft.

This is a standard Android ongoing notification. It can’t be swiped away while running, and its text is the app’s own status line (RTST’s “Downloading voice…”, Dino’s “Getting the … voice ready”). Its only action is Cancel, which stops the download like the in-app Cancel, with nothing restarting by itself. Tapping the notification opens the page where the download started. In Dino, that is behind the gate. The notification goes away by itself when the pack is ready, fails or is cancelled. No “done” notification is left behind. Each app shows one notification per download. Both are shown here only to compare. iOS has no notification. The download just finishes in the background.
