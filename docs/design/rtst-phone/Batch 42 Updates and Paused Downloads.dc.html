This shows when the installed version is below android.minVersion (or ios.minVersion). It replaces the whole app, with no back, close or skip, and Android’s back button closes the app. It works like Dino’s frame 61, but without the gate, because RTST’s users are grown-ups. This shows when iOS’s background session ends a download with a network error while the app is in the background. The text is the same as N2. Because it uses provisional authorisation, there’s no permission prompt. It arrives silently in Notification Center only, with no banner, sound or lock-screen alert. iOS adds its own Keep… / Turn Off… buttons the first time, and the app can’t change them. Tapping it opens the app, and the foreground resume finishes the download. If notifications are off, nothing is posted, and opening the app still resumes it. Dino posts the same notification with its own icon. The copy is a draft.
