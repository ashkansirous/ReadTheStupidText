# The Three Little Pigs: art record

Pilot book for the `create-dino-story` skill (Slice 119). Bundled starter, 6 pages.

**Style:** Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolour and coloured pencil, warm gentle
colours, simple rounded shapes, no text or letters anywhere. Portrait 3:4, 896x1200.

## Characters (canonical, from the accepted line-up)

Line-up: [`three-little-pigs/characters/lineup.jpg`](three-little-pigs/characters/lineup.jpg)
(character chat: https://gemini.google.com/app/5bdec8c40a5d27ba).

- **Pip:** small round pink pig; yellow straw hat with a blue band and a blue flower; yellow scarf. No
  shirt, no trousers.
- **Pop:** slightly taller, thinner pink pig; blue-and-white striped T-shirt under green dungarees.
- **Pim:** sturdy pink pig; plain red knitted beanie (no bobble, not a cap); blue shirt with rolled-up
  sleeves; small trowel. No trousers.
- **Wolf:** tall grey wolf, about twice the pigs' height, big pointy ears, bushy tail; scruffy blue
  waistcoat with patches. Silly, never scary: closed-mouth grin, no tongue, no bared teeth.
