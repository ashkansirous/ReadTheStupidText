iPhone Duo: the outer screen and the open book
Every current Dino screen on both of the Duo’s displays: the corner (Books, Store with the subscription, Settings), the kids’ shelf, reading, recording, the back cover, the grown-ups’ gate and the new onboarding. The older batch pages are history and are not redrawn. The rule is the one we already have, the 600 split: the outer screen is under 600 wide, so it uses the phone layouts; the inner screen is 600 or wider, so it uses the rail and two-pane layouts, with one addition the Duo earns: the reading pages become a real open book, picture on the left page, words on the right, the fold as the gutter.
What Apple has published: a 7.6-inch inner display (7.58″ diagonal) and a 5.4-inch outer one (5.36″), with the same aspect ratio; a book-style fold; the Dynamic Island turned vertical, on the side of both screens; a status bar that sits in a corner; the inner FaceTime camera under the display; Split View with two apps side by side.
What it hasn’t (checked 2026-11-16): pixel resolutions, points, safe areas and which side the island sits on. Two screens with one aspect ratio, where the big one is the small one doubled, only works at √2 (A4-like). That gives the sizes drawn here, all estimates: outer about 433 × 613 pt, inner about 867 × 613 pt, a 40 pt strip for the island on the left. Replace them with Xcode’s Device Hub numbers when Apple ships Duo support.
What that means for Dino: the outer screen is exactly a 393-wide phone after the island strip, but only 613 tall (shorter than an iPhone 8’s 667), so the phone screens fit across but show less down. The inner screen is landscape, wider than a phone in landscape and the height of the outer one, so the corner keeps its ink rail and the shelf its cream rail, like a tablet in landscape.

Under 600 wide: tabs along the bottom, one column, the coral button pinned at the foot. Questions for the review
1. Island side. Apple says the Dynamic Island is vertical “on the side” but not which side or how far down. Drawn on the left with a 40 pt strip. When Xcode has the Duo, the safe areas replace these guesses; the layouts don’t change.
2. Split View. The Duo can run Dino beside another app. Half of the inner screen is about 433 wide, so Dino then shows its outer-screen layouts. Nothing extra to design, but worth a device check.
