Four questions for the review
1 · Tag colours. Each tag gets its own soft fill with dark text (162), all from existing tones. Hot is coral, so it’s the loudest. Is that right, or should Recommended lead?
2 · Cancel while the first books download (165). Drawn: back to the picker with the ticks kept, and a book that already finished stays. Or should Cancel throw both away?
3 · Intro art (168). With no starters in the app, the intro’s pictures can’t use book art. Drawn with Dino and plain colour blocks. Or should the art come from the books the parent just picked?
4 · iPhone page reader (170). If iOS uses Apple’s built-in reader, nothing downloads there and 170–171 are Android only. The slice says to check this first.
