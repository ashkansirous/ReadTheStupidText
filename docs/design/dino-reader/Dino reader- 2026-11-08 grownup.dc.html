Kids’ shelf, same skeleton
The shelf uses the corner’s layout with the colour swapped: cream instead of ink, no tabs. The switch between the two places is always in the same spot: on phones, the pill top right (Grown-ups here, Child’s shelf there); on wide screens, the button under the name in the rail. Fix: “My stories” was being cut to “Stories…” beside the Dino icon. Now the title is the first thing to give way: it switches to a short form (“Stories”), then hides; the Dino icon stays as the shelf’s mark. The Grown-ups pill always keeps its word. The language button is a flag of the current story language on every screen, never text: in the header row with the title: left of the pill on phones, at the right end of the title row in the wide rails. The Grown-ups button keeps its full row.
