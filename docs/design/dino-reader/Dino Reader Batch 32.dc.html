Dino Reader · Batch 32 (Slice 155) · Decision 160
A three-step intro that does the real thing
The language page and the voice download stay as they are (Batch 16, frames 16–17). On “Ready to read!” the Set up the stories button now starts three steps: store, record, add. Reading a book needs no step, since the shelf is right there. Each step has one message, one picture, Try it (opens the real screen) and Skip. Coming back from a feature moves on to the next step. Skip all and the last step end on the existing “Ready for my child” hand-over. There is no grown-up gate during the intro, and there are no coach-marks or tips. Only new installs see it. Grown-up mode can replay it.
