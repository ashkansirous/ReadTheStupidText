Dino Reader · First run and intro · every screen size
Onboarding in the shelf’s skeleton
The flow is Batch 48’s: language → first books → (getting the books / offline) → Ready to read, then the intro two places → read → record → languages (“of 4”). Add a book and the voice download are gone. The layouts follow the same 600 rule as the shelf and corner.
Under 600 wide: one column. Picture or heading on top, then what you tap, with the main coral button pinned at the bottom above the home bar.
600 or wider: two panes. The left pane is the shelf rail’s cream wash and holds the picture or heading, never a button. The right pane holds everything you tap, with the coral button at its foot.
Waits, offline and Ready to read are one centred column (max 440) on every size: a pause, not a step.
One question for the review
Flags for languages. Every language button now has a flag, as asked. Some languages don’t have one obvious flag: English (UK or US), Português (Portugal or Brazil), العربية (drawn as plain green, no country), فارسی (drawn as the plain tricolour, no emblem) and 中文. Families who read these may feel strongly about the choice. Keep these flags, use the phone’s region to pick one, or show a globe for these five?
