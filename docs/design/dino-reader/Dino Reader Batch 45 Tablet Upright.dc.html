Tablets held upright, and the back cover
Drawn on top of Batch 44. A tablet held upright (short side ≥ 600 dp, portrait) gets one rule per screen: stack, don’t shrink. Picture on top, work below, controls in a row at the foot; the phone’s boxes become centred cards sized for a tablet. Light only, never mirrored (Slice 96). Frames 133–150. Tablet frames are 804 × 1164 dp (iPad Air, 1×); 150 is a 10″ Android at 800 × 1280.
For the review
1 · Four columns on an iPad Air? At 804 dp, Batch 44’s ÷ 190 gives 3. I used a tablet divisor of 170 so it gives 4 (covers ~166 dp, still bigger than a phone’s). 3 on small 600–680 dp tablets.
2 · Reading picture height. The picture panel takes 48% of the height and the art is fitted whole inside it (3:4 art leaves cream margins left and right). Or crop to fill the width?
3 · Back cover. Built in Slice 237 without a mock. 147–149 draw it upright, in landscape and in Farsi; the build should be checked against them.
Layout rules for Core (extend Slice 232’s)
IsTabletPortrait(w,h): h ≥ w and w ≥ 600 dp.
ShelfLayout: tablet portrait → columns = clamp(⌊(w − 80) ÷ 170⌋, 3, 6). Side padding 40 dp.
ReadingLayout.Stacked: picture panel = 48% of h, art fitted whole; text below at 32 sp (36 in text only); controls row 64 / 84 / 64.
CardWidth: sheets, gate and first run become centred cards: min(w − 80, 560 / 520 / 620).
SidePanes: w ≥ 744 → corner rail (300) and Settings two panes, as 126 and 128.
Lists (Store, Cast) go to two columns; Backup to two cards side by side.
