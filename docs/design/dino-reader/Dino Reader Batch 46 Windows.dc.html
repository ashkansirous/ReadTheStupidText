Dino on Windows: a real desktop window
Today the window opens at WinUI’s default size, shrinks to anything, and only answers touch. This batch gives it a size, a minimum, a title bar in Dino’s colours, and keyboard, hover, focus, wheel and tooltips on every page. The layouts are the phone and tablet ones from Batch 44 and Batch 45, picked by the window’s size: no Windows-only layouts. Light only, never mirrored. Frames 151–162, drawn at 1 dp = 1 px (100% scaling).
For the review
1 · Minimum 800 × 560, not 720 × 540. At 720 the reading card next to a whole picture is ~240 dp wide (about 18 letters a line at 22 sp). 800 × 560 keeps 113’s proportions (153). Opening size 1100 × 760 as planned.
2 · The gate sum by keyboard: type the answer and press Enter (158), rather than number keys 1–6 for the six buttons, so a child mashing keys gets no easier guess than tapping.
3 · Title bar: cream on kids’ pages, paper on grown-up pages, with Windows’ own caption buttons (the red close hover is Windows’, not ours).
Window → layout (Core, unit-tested)
WindowLayout(w, h) on the content area (window minus the 32 px title bar):
• h > w and w ≥ 600 → tablet upright (Batch 45).
• w ≥ h and w < 960 → phone landscape rules (Batch 44, 110–124), except the shelf is a grid, not a sideways row.
• w ≥ 960 → tablet landscape (125–130). Above 1600 content is capped at 1600 and centred.
Opening 1100 × 760 → tablet landscape. Minimum 800 × 560 → phone landscape. Maximised 1920 × 1080 → tablet landscape, capped.
The window remembers size, place and maximised state; if the saved place is off every screen it opens centred on the main one.
