Dino Reader · Batch 10 (Slices 58–67) · ages 3–8
A voice for every character, a highlight on every page
The app works out who is speaking and gives each character their own voice for the whole book, with a narrator voice the grown-up picks. Home is a gallery of the child's stories. Reading has two modes the child can switch at any time: picture (the real scanned page, or a generated book page, with the spoken sentence boxed) and text. Pages follow the reading; the child can wander and tap a sentence to read from there. Everything that changes the library sits behind hold 3 s + a simple sum. Everything stays on the device.
Fonts (bundle in the app, list the licences in STORE.md): Fredoka for the interface, Andika for story text, Vazirmatn for Farsi and Arabic. All three are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Chinese and Japanese use the system font. Pages are never tilted, so highlight boxes always sit on the words.
