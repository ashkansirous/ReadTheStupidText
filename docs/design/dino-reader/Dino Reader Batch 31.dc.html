After the tester review: three views, slower reading, a simpler cast page
The reading top bar gets a three-way view switch (Slice 151). It replaces the “Show the words over the pictures” switch (frames 57–58, Batch 25). The cast page loses Merge two characters and every mention of the animal (Slices 151, 152; Decision 157 reverses 132, so frame 59 in Batch 26–28 is superseded). Grown-up settings get a Reading speed row (Slice 150). Slice 148’s mid-read stall uses the Batch 30 overlay. Farsi and Arabic books may show vowel marks (Slice 154), but only if the listening test passes. Slice 153 (robotic voices) changes no screens; its listening page is a dev tool.
