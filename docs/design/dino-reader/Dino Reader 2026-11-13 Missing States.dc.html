The screens the plan needs and no mock drew
Four groups: the drawn back cover with the credits over it, a Store book that can’t be got (and the Buy, Restore and Redeem buttons that shipped without a mock), the reading page when a book’s sound is missing, and the Windows grown-ups’ gate. Everything uses the 2026-11-08 skeleton (corner and shelf) and the shipped reading page (Batch 50). Phones are drawn at iPhone 15 Pro size (393 × 852).
1 · Slice 359
The back cover: a drawn picture, the credits over its middle
The drawn back cover fills the picture area, at every size and in every view (picture, picture and text, text only: the back cover is a picture page, so the view switch hides here as it did on Slice 237’s page). The credits sit on a cream card in the middle, so they stay readable whatever the picture does behind them. The pictures are told to keep the middle calm, and the card is there in case one doesn’t. Nothing is read aloud and nothing is counted. Read again replaces Play and next is dimmed, as on the old page. The picture here is a stand-in, the last Lion and Mouse page: the real one is sample 2 (bedtime), copied in by step 2.

4 · Windows (and every wide screen)
The grown-ups’ gate: one card in the middle
Frame 158 split the gate across the window: words on the left, the ring or the keypad pushed to the far right edge, with a big empty gap between them. Now both steps are one cream card in the middle of the window, over the dimmed shelf. The thing you act on (the ring, the answer field and keys) sits in the middle of the card, under its heading, so the eye and the mouse go straight down. The card is the same at every width of 600 or more: Windows, tablets and phone landscape. Phones held upright keep the bottom sheet as built.
