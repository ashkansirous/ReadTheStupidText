One shelf with a lock, and a shorter onboarding
The kids’ shelf and the grown-ups’ corner become one screen. The Child’s shelf pill and the Grown-ups button at the top are gone. The bottom menu is Books · Store · Settings · Lock, and it starts locked: a child can open a book to read and change the reading language, and nothing else. The lock opens with today’s grown-ups popup (hold 3 seconds, then the sum). Tapping it while unlocked locks at once, and it locks itself after 30 seconds without a grown-up tap. Onboarding loses the second-language step and the corner tour, and the recording step gets a Skip. This replaces the corner header (ink bar and coral pill) on every earlier page; their content (Store states, Settings rows, subscription) stays as drawn.
1 · The rules
What the lock allows, and when it closes
Locked (the default, and after every app start): Books shows the covers with no ••• menu; tapping a cover reads it. The reading-language button works, on the shelf and on the reading page. Store and Settings are faded; tapping them shows a hint and pulses the lock. Book options, reordering, recording, buying, restoring and every setting are out of reach.
Unlocking: tap the lock, then today’s popup: hold 3 seconds, then the sum. Cancel leaves it locked. Unlocked: the last tab becomes a bigger, clearly open lock in brand purple (“Unlocked”), the title gets an “Unlocked · 24s” chip, covers get their ••• button, Store and Settings open. Tapping a cover still reads the book.
Locking: tap the lock (no question), or 30 seconds without a grown-up tap. Grown-up taps are: Store, Settings, a ••• menu, anything inside them. Reading, turning pages, changing the reading language and not touching the phone do not count, so the timer keeps running. When it locks: any sheet closes, Store or Settings goes back to Books, and a short “Locked again” message shows. The store’s own buying sheet and a recording in progress hold the timer until they finish.
