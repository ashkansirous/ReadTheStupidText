Dino Reader · 2026-11-15 · Slice 384 (subscription), with Slice 394’s age chips
The subscription, in every state the store can be in
Slice 384 was built (#1174) with no mock: one subscription card at the top of the Store (title, line, two “Try free, then …” buttons, terms, links, Manage), greyed books with Subscribe again and Buy this book on the parents’ shelf, and those books hidden from the child. This page draws the built screens and the states the build has no screen for: trial already used, the free trial running, a failed payment (the store’s grace period), the subscription ended, and the offline 3-day rule. Built strings are used as they are; new strings are marked new in the notes. The age chips follow Slice 394: only bands with books, so “9–12” is gone. The Store’s rows, errors and Restore/Redeem card are the 2026-11-13 Missing States ones.
1 · The Store tab
One card above the books, five states
The card is always the first thing under the chips, so a parent sees the offer before the books and their single prices. Chips and the author row stay above it, so filtering never hides the card. Plans: yearly first and primary (better value for a family), monthly second. The price in each button is the store’s own string, never written in the app.

4 · Restore purchases and Redeem a code
Out of the bottom of the Store, into the three places people look
The shipped “Bought books before?” card sits at the very bottom of the Store list, after every book, so nobody finds it, and moving it in and out of the list’s footer is also the likely cause of the “9–12” crash (Slice 394). The card goes. Restore and Redeem live where someone actually goes looking: (1) a Restore purchases link on the Store’s first line, always visible above the chips and the subscription card; (2) the Books and buying group in Settings (SB9), which holds Restore and Redeem a code with a line saying what each does; (3) right where it is needed: a book that answers “You need to buy this book first”, or a search that found nothing. Results show as a short dark message above the tab bar, never at the end of a list. Every Store frame above (SB1–SB5, SB10) already has the new first line.

Where the question comes up: the 402 strip under a book (“You need to buy this book first.”) gets a new link, “Bought it already? Restore purchases”, which runs the same restore. The same link sits on the parents’ greyed books when a lapse may be a lost purchase rather than an ended subscription (SB6’s banner gains it under “See plans in the Store”).

Questions for the review
1. “Save 37%” on the yearly plan. Worked out from the store’s two prices on the device. Stores allow it, but it is a new claim on screen. Show it, or leave the yearly button plain?
2. Yearly first. Drawn as the primary button. Prefer monthly first (cheaper-looking entry) instead?
3. Subscribe from onboarding. The new onboarding (2026-11-13) mentions the Store in the corner tour only. Should the tour’s Store tip name the subscription (“…or every book with one subscription”)? Drawn there now as a one-line change.
