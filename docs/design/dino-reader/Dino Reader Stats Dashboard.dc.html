Downloads are store-book downloads from the apps. A listen is a book played for 30 seconds or more. Hours are real played time from the app; for speakers without reported time, the book’s length (hatched, estimate). Weeks start Monday, UTC. Data from {{ dataFrom }}; kept daily in D1 after Analytics Engine drops raw rows.
Filters. Four fixed periods, no custom dates; the chart’s subtitle names the exact days. “Today” allows only day bars; “This year” starts on week bars. Platforms are chips in their chart colour; at least one stays on. Alexa is shown but off and dimmed until Slice 282 ships (“not live yet”). The book picker lists store titles from /api/stories plus “Own books”.
Numbers and chart. The three tiles are the chart’s switch, so there is one chart, not three. Bars stack by platform in a fixed order (Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Sonos, Alexa). iPhone and iPad are split by the batch’s form factor (blob12, phone or tablet, Slice 166), so the rollup keys iOS rows as iphone or ipad. iOS rows with no form factor (builds before Slice 166) count under iPhone with a one-line note in the hover; Android keeps one chip although tablets are known too. Sonos hours are hatched because they are estimates (Slice 289). When the period starts before listens were counted, a note says from when (Slice 288’s release); earlier bars are empty, not zero-height errors. Hover a bar for its exact numbers.
States. A wait over 500 ms greys the chart with “Loading…” and keeps the old numbers; an error shows one line with Try again. On a phone the tiles stack and the table keeps Book and the sorted column.
