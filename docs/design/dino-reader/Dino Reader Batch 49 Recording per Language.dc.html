Record in each story language, hear it in that language
The record intro gets a Record in row with the family’s story languages only, the app language first and picked. Everything after it, the card text, the pieces, the counts, Listen and change, is that language’s. Reading works as for any book: the language button is back, and a recorded piece plays only in the language it was recorded in. Recordings made before this slice belong to the app’s main language. Drawn on iPhone 8 (375 × 667), English app with Farsi as a side language. Frames 192–196 change 178, 182, 188 and 189 from Batch 48.
Two questions for the review
1 · How you record, per language? Drawn: one character at a time or whole pages is picked per language, as are the ticks, so English can be whole pages and Farsi just the Wolf. Or one choice for the whole book?
2 · Which language opens? Drawn: the intro always opens on the app language. Or on the language last recorded in this book?
