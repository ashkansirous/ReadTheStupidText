Dino Reader · First run, redone · 2026-11-13 · no slice yet
Onboarding that shows what Dino can do
One guided path, every step required, in six parts: language → reading languages → first books → listen (voices and feelings, in each language) → your voice (record Pim, hear yourself) → the grown-ups’ corner and the child’s shelf. It replaces the 2026-11-08 first run and its separate intro (two places, read, record, languages). A step bar at the top always says where you are. Nothing can be skipped; each step’s Continue wakes up when the step is done. Drawn at iPhone 15 Pro size. 600 or wider follows the same two-pane rule as 2026-11-08 (W frames at the end).
Part 1 · Steps 1 to 3
Language, the family’s reading languages, the first books
Step 1 is the app’s language. Step 2 always comes next and can’t be skipped, but it can be left as it is: the app language is already ticked, so Continue is live from the start. Its job is to explain why a family would pick two or three. Step 3 needs at least two books, as now; the getting-the-books wait and the offline screen that follow are the 2026-11-08 ones, unchanged.

Questions for the review
1. Pim’s recording after onboarding. Drawn: the two clips stay on The Three Little Pigs, so the child hears the parent as Pim from day one, and N7 offers Remove my voice. The other way: always remove them when onboarding ends and treat N6–N7 as a pure demo. Decided 2026-11-13: removed when onboarding ends, and the demo uses one set page per free book.
2. Microphone refused. Recording can’t be skipped, but a parent may refuse the microphone. Proposed: N6 then shows “Dino needs the microphone to record. You can allow it in Settings.” with Open settings and a quiet Continue without recording, the only way past step 5 without a recording. Decided 2026-11-13: yes.
