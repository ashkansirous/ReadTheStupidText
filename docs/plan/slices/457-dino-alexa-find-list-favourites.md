## Why

The owner, 2026-11-26, on a real Echo: "It starts ok, but it gives the list of 5 stories out of all but then cannot find them. It said goldylock which was its first suggestion, it said I cannot find it. I said goldylock and the 3 bears, it said it cannot find it. I said the lion and the mouse, it started reading. I said alexa the lion, it started audibale even though it was in dino. It did not suggest buying any subscription and not suggested any list or search command. It did not check with likeliness to search, like goldy or goldylock should be enough". Then: "we also need to add favorites in case a kid likes a story and parents want to play that".

What was found the same day (three sources):
- **Code** (origin/main db6113ed): `worker/alexa.ts` `findBooks` (:205-214) matches whole words only (exact, then a word run, then 75% of words), with no spelling tolerance; "goldylock" scores 0 against "goldilocks" and "3" is not "three". The interaction model has one custom intent, `ReadBookIntent`, whose `book` slot is `AMAZON.SearchQuery` behind a carrier word ("read", "play" ...), so a bare title reaches no intent. The launch reads the first 5 store titles (`MAX_LISTED_BOOKS`, :21) and names no command; Help repeats the launch line. No list intent, no favourites.
- **Simulation** (`ask smapi simulate-skill`, en-GB, development stage): "goldilocks", "goldylock", "goldilocks and the three bears", "what books do you have" each failed with "did not resolve to any intent"; "read goldilocks" resolved and returned `AudioPlayer.Play`.
- **Live store and logs:** of the 16 English books in `/api/stories`, only 3 listening tracks play (`/api/stories/<id>/listen/en` answers 206 for The Lion and the Mouse, X Marks the Treasure! and The Gingerbread Man); 12 answer 402 (paid) and The Three Little Pigs answers 404. Four of the five books the launch offers are paid, so the skill sends Alexa a Play for a track that refuses it (this is also why only The Lion and the Mouse played). The Worker's own logs show every Echo request answered 200; they record no intent or outcome, so a miss cannot be read from them.
- "Alexa, the lion" during playback went to Audible: an AudioPlayer skill must end its session when the story starts, so a bare "Alexa, …" goes to Alexa itself. No skill can take that over; only "Alexa, ask Dino Bedtime Stories for …" and the built-in audio words (pause, resume, previous, and the child's "repeat", "next", "shuffle" of decision 12) reach the skill.
- A subscription offer is Slice 415 item D (Alexa In-Skill Purchasing, kid skill), not this slice.

## Decisions

Claude, 2026-11-26 (obvious behaviour, from the owner's report):
1. **A book is found from any recognisable part of its name, with spelling slack.** Number words and digits are the same ("3" = "three"), plural and singular are the same, small spelling slips match (edit distance 1 for words of 4-7 letters, 2 for longer), and a first-word prefix of 4+ letters matches ("goldy" → Goldilocks). When two books match equally, the skill names both and asks which.
2. **A bare title works, with no carrier word.** A second intent takes the title alone, using a custom slot type of every book's name plus short names ("goldilocks", "three bears", "the lion", "gingerbread man"), generated from the repo's English book metadata by `tools/dino/alexa-book-names.mjs`; the carrier-word `SearchQuery` intent stays for names not in the list. A test fails when a repo book has no slot value.
3. **Only books that play are offered as playable.** The launch says how many books there are, names the free ones first, then says the rest come with the Dino subscription. A book whose track would refuse (402) or is missing (404) is never sent as an `AudioPlayer.Play`: a paid book gets a short spoken "this one comes with the Dino subscription in the Dino app" and the free list (Slice 415 decision 2 wording rules apply once 415 builds the link); a missing track is left out of every list.
4. **Every reply says what you can say next.** Launch: "Say a book's name, say 'more books', or say 'play my favourites'." Help lists all commands, including "Alexa, ask Dino Bedtime Stories for <book>" while a story plays; the words for the child after a story are decision 12's ("repeat", "next", "shuffle"), said in Help and in the launch, "shuffle" only when the account has a playable favourite.
5. **A list command pages through every book**, 5 at a time ("what books do you have", "list the books", "more books", "what else"), then "that's all the books".
6. **Favourites, per Alexa account.** "Add this to my favourites" (also as "Alexa, ask Dino Bedtime Stories to save this story" during or after playback, using the current or last AudioPlayer token), "play my favourites" (plays them in a row, next/previous move through them), "what are my favourites", "remove <book> from my favourites". Stored in the `STATS` D1 database keyed by the opaque Alexa user id, nothing else; the privacy text says so. Launch mentions favourites only when the account has some.
7. **Logs record the outcome, never what was said.** Each request logs intent name and outcome (found <book id> / not found / paid / list page / favourite action). The child's words are never logged (child-directed skill); misses are read from the Alexa console's anonymised utterance history instead.

Owner, 2026-11-26 (RefineScope answers):
8. **Paid books** (decision 3): "agree, but I think we have 4 free books". Live check 2026-11-26: 4 tracks play (The Lion and the Mouse, X Marks the Treasure!, The Gingerbread Man, and The Three Little Pigs, whose track now answers 206). The launch and lists take the free set from the store data, never a fixed number.
9. **Favourites are one list per Amazon account**, shared by the household ("agree"); no per-voice-profile lists.
10. **When a story finishes, the skill asks what to play next with three choices:** "after it finishes, it should ask, what do you want me to play next? and give 3 suggestions. a- repeat the story again? b- random one from their fav stories c- a new one from available stories". So: (a) the same story again, (b) a random favourite (offered only when the account has favourites), (c) a playable book not just played. How this is done within Alexa's limits on speaking after audio ends is item 7; the owner accepted item 7's end-of-story clip in place of Alexa asking and listening (2026-11-26: "yes, it is ok").
12. **The end-of-story words are one word a 3-year-old can say** (owner, 2026-11-26, on the first clip text: "start over? ask Dino Bedtime Stories for a favourite? for a 3 years old? ... say somehting simple like "repeat" and "favorate" simple 1 words that a kid acan say"). Amazon routes only its built-in audio words to the skill without its name (standard built-in intents page, read 2026-11-26: Repeat, StartOver, Next, Previous, Resume, Loop, Shuffle), and a custom word like "favourite" cannot reach it without "ask Dino Bedtime Stories" (name-free interaction is not open to store skills). So: **"Alexa, repeat"** = the same story again, **"Alexa, next"** = a new story, **"Alexa, shuffle"** = a random favourite. The clip says only: "Again? Say: Alexa, repeat. A new story? Say: Alexa, next." plus, with favourites, "A favourite? Say: Alexa, shuffle." "Start over", "loop" and "again" variants do the same as repeat; the named "play a favourite" stays for parents but is never in the clip.
11. **Alexa subscription price** (Slice 415, recorded there): same as the app, £1.99 a month or £14.99 a year, no free trial ("agree").

(built 2026-11-26: two AAC clips, narrator voice Hali, rendered with `tools/dino/premade-audio/generate.py --batch` from a two-sentence dump and a narrator-only cast, served as site assets `site/dino/alexa-clips/what-next.m4a` and `what-next-with-favourites.m4a` (URL `/dino/alexa-clips/<file>` on both hosts, no upload step; clip token `v1|<book>|en|<version>|0|c`); new `PlayFavouriteIntent` for "play a favourite" / "for a favourite") "What next?" after a story (decision 10), within Alexa's AudioPlayer limits. Amazon docs (AudioPlayer interface reference, standard built-in intents; read 2026-11-26): after audio ends a skill cannot speak, reprompt or open the microphone (PlaybackFinished takes only Stop/ClearQueue; no session), but on PlaybackNearlyFinished it may ENQUEUE another audio item, and "start over", "next" and "previous" reach the skill without its name while it is the most recent skill to play audio. So:
  - On PlaybackNearlyFinished of a single book, ENQUEUE a short pre-recorded spoken clip in the Dino narrator voice: "Again? Say: Alexa, repeat. A new story? Say: Alexa, next. A favourite? Say: Alexa, shuffle." (slow and clear, short pauses between the sentences; decision 12 replaces the first wording). A second clip without the favourite sentence is used when the account has no favourites. Clips are rendered once with the repo's own voice tooling, stored next to the other served audio, and their token carries the book just played.
  - "Repeat" (also loop on, start over) during or after the clip replays that book (a), and restarts a book that is playing; "shuffle" plays a random favourite (b), or a new playable book when there are no favourites; "next" plays a playable book not just played (c). "Play a favourite" stays for parents but is not in the clip; loop off and shuffle off do nothing.
  - Favourites played in a row (decision 6) keep playing to the end of the list, then the clip.
