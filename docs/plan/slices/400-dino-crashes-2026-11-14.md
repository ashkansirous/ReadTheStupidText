## Why

1. **Android 1.123.1 (store), 2026-11-14 21:03Z, 2 reports:** `Java.Lang.RuntimeException` (Exception_WasThrown) thrown by `Java.IO.InputStream.Close` inside `InputStreamInvoker.Dispose`, `BufferedStream.Dispose`, `DelegatingStream.Dispose`. 2. **iOS 1.131.0 (TestFlight), 2026-11-15 15:21Z, 1 report:** MetricKit `native`, exception 10 signal 9, `0x8BADF00D` (the scene-update watchdog: the main thread was busy for 10 s of wall clock). Id `658ad15c-0a14-4b6e-b686-6307df1fe863`. An `unclean_exit` for 1.131.0 came the same day. No frames of our code.
3. **`unclean_exit`:** 26 reports in the window, about 18 on Android store builds 1.94.0 to 1.129.2 (newest 2026-11-16 on 1.129.2) and 5 on iOS. The cause is unknown; Play and Apple matched only a few.

One user report, `report-T7RSG327` (iOS 1.117.0, 2026-11-08, no stack), is already named in `plan.md`'s rolling-log bullet.

No cause is proven. Anything below that names a code path is "likely" until a log or a rerun shows it.
