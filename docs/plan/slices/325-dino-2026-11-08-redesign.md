**Why.** The user updated the Dino Reader design on 2026-11-08 and asked for one slice that brings every page of the
app to it (user, 2026-11-08: "add a slice to update all pages with the new design"). The mocks are copied in
`docs/design/dino-reader/` (README section "2026-11-08 redesign (Slice 325)"):

- `Dino reader- 2026-11-08 grownup.dc.html` — the grown-ups corner as three tabs (Books, Store, Settings) with the
  coral way back in the ink header, and the kids shelf to match, on every screen size (P/L/T/W and K frames).

**The mock trumps this plan and the shipped app** (`~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, *Claude Design*): build exactly what the
frames show.

**Platforms.** Android, iOS and the Windows head (the mock has Windows frames). One MAUI code path serves all three; each part lists what differs per platform.

**Pages this slice restyles (the pages the two mocks draw).** Grown-ups' corner: Books tab, Store tab, Settings tab (the list; the sub-pages sit inside it with the tab chrome around them). Kids' shelf. First run: language, first books, getting the books, offline, ready. Intro: two places, read, record, languages.

**Pages left out (the mocks do not draw them, so they keep their shipped look and only gain the new frame where noted).** The grown-up gate (`GatePage`); the reading page (`ReadingPage`); the book sheet (`BookSheet`); the record intro and record pages; the shelf's reorder mode (`ShelfReorderView`); the update page (`UpdatePage`); the language-pick menu (`StoryLanguageMenu`). #### Design (Dino 2026-11-08 grown-up and onboarding mocks, added 2026-11-08)

Two files, about 40 frames. The mock does **not** agree with the plan or the shipped app: it replaces the corner's side rail, its gear, its pushed Store and Settings pages (Slice 229's Done on both), the two-pane Settings (Slice 232 part D), the tablet shelf's four columns (Slice 234), the first-run card and 3-column grid (Slices 232 part E, 234), the intro's wording and the first-run voice line. Values below are read from the markup; "G 56" means line 56 of the grown-up file, "O 38" line 38 of the onboarding file. Colours are the existing `App.xaml` tokens: ink `#2b2540` (`Ink`), cream `#FFF6E6` (`Cream`), sand `#F3EFE6` (`Sand`), brand `#5B57E8` (`Brand`), tint `#ECE7FF` (`BrandTint`), coral `#FF7A59` with shadow `0 Npx 0 #E2583A` (`Coral`), body `#5a5470` (`Body`), muted `#8a8399` (`Muted`), amber `#FFC24D`, rail text `#cfc6e6`, hairline `#e8e1d3` / `#efe9dd` (`Hairline`), green `#2E7D32`, rail wash `rgba(43,37,64,.05)`, pill fill `rgba(43,37,64,.07)` (the same 7% as `ControlFill`). The font is the app's Fredoka (`DinoFonts.Ui`), Vazirmatn for Persian and Arabic text. The mock loads Fredoka and Vazirmatn from Google Fonts for the canvas only; that is canvas furniture, not built (the app bundles both). The status bars, phone bezels and the dark home-bar strokes are canvas furniture too.

**The one layout rule (G 20, O 20-25):** under 600 wide the corner's tabs go along the bottom; 600 or wider (phone landscape, tablets, Windows) the ink becomes a left rail holding the name, the way back, then the tabs. The shelf follows the same split with a cream rail and no tabs. Onboarding under 600 is one column (picture or heading on top, what you tap below, the coral button pinned at the foot); 600 or wider is two panes (left pane = the cream wash with the picture or heading, never a button; right pane = everything you tap, the coral button at its foot). Waits, offline and Ready are one centred column, max 440, at every size. This reuses `LandscapeLayout.TabletShortSide` (600); Windows never goes below 800 (`WindowLayout.MinimumWindowWidth`).
