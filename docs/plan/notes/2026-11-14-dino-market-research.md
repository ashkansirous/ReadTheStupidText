# Dino Reader market research (2026-11-14)

Done in another session on 2026-11-14 from App Store, Google Play and vendor pages: about 50 apps, English stores plus
Farsi, Arabic, German, Japanese and Nordic ones. Used by the repo skill `.claude/skills/store-listing/` (step 0) and
Slice 291 items 7-9. Prices are as seen that day; re-check before quoting any of them.

## What only Dino does

No app found combines all four: a different voice per character, a family member recording **one character**,
many languages, and buying single books. The research said "each competitor has at most one of these"; by its own
list Readmio has two (whole-book family recording and 13 languages), so the safe claim is "none combines all four".

## Competitors by feature

- **Family recording, whole book only:** Readmio (13 languages, $5.99/month, $39.99/year or $159 lifetime), Readby
  ($1.99/month), Vooks ($9.99/month or $69.99/year), Dreambook Kids, Story Time Together, Eliozz, Kidzify (AI voice
  clone), Yonde (JP). My Story (SE) records per page, but only in books the child makes.
- **Character voices, no family recording:** Arden (iOS, "10 character voices", English only, $1.99/month), StoryTime
  Buddy (AI, 4 languages). Neither is an illustrated library.
- **Multilingual libraries, one narrator, subscription:** BookaBooka (about 19 languages incl. Farsi and Arabic,
  $4.49/month, the closest on languages), Booka (6), Lylli (SE, 4 languages, $9.99/month or $75.99/year), Pickatale
  (£5.99/month, 79 kr/month), Polyglutt/Polylino (Swedish, sold to preschools and libraries), Epic (40,000 books,
  subscription).
- **Farsi** (Cafe Bazaar: Qesseh Shab, Ay Ghesse): 500+ audio-only stories, no pictures, no character voices.
- **Arabic:** single-language apps (I Read Arabic, Hekayat Belaraby, Inklingo).

## Payment

Almost every competitor is a monthly subscription. Readmio (subscription + lifetime) and Dreambook (subscription +
packs) mix payment types, but none sells single books the family keeps. Dino today: 4 free books, the rest bought
one at a time and kept (Slice 346). Planned, not built: £1.99/month or £14.99/year with a 7-day free trial, alongside
single books (Slice 384, `docs/plan/slices/384-dino-subscription.md`; its Decisions win). That is at the cheap end of
the subscription prices above.

## Weak spot

15 books against hundreds or thousands. The listing never sells quantity; it sells quality (classic tales, retold
gently, hand-picked).

## Caveats

Epic checked on its home page only; PIBO, Onilo and some Arabic apps from search results only; Bookful and Moonbeam
not found; the Amazon Kids+ page failed to load.
