Narrated bedtime stories & picture books for kids. Record your own voice too.

## Full description (<= 4,000)

Bedtime stories for kids, picture books read aloud: Dino is a story app for children's stories in which every character has its own voice. The narrator tells the story, the wolf and the little pigs each sound like themselves, and a parent or grandparent can record their own voice as any character: Grandma can be the wolf.

Every story comes in 15 languages, for families who keep a home language next to the school language: Farsi, Arabic, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish and more. A grown-up picks two or three story languages, and the child can switch between them while reading, hearing the same story and learning the words in both.

No ads. No account. A downloaded story reads offline, and your recordings stay on your device.

Four illustrated stories are free: The Lion and the Mouse, The Gingerbread Man, The Three Little Pigs and X Marks the Treasure. Subscribe for every book, or buy only the books you want and keep them. The subscription is monthly or yearly, starts with a 7-day free trial (once per Google account), renews until you cancel it in Google Play, and opens every paid book while it runs. When it ends, books you only had through it leave the child's shelf; subscribe again or buy one and it comes straight back. Books you buy are yours to keep, with or without a subscription. A grown-up buys behind the grown-ups gate and can restore purchases at any time.

FOR CHILDREN AGED 3 TO 9
Children listen, look at the pictures and follow along: good for bedtime stories, and for bilingual children learning words in two languages. A grown-up sets it up, picks the books and records voices behind a grown-ups gate.

CLASSIC TALES, RETOLD GENTLY
Hand-picked picture books, each retold for young children, with a picture on every page.

MORE TO ENJOY
- Each sentence lights up as it is read.
- Picture only, text only, or picture and text.
- Reading speed from 0.8x to 1.2x, and animal sounds you can switch on or off.
- A night theme for bedtime.
- Record any character, on any page, in any story language; back up and restore your recordings.

LANGUAGES
English, Chinese, Spanish, Arabic, French, Portuguese, Russian, German, Japanese, Farsi, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Finnish and Norwegian: the app, the stories and the voices.

SAFE AND PRIVATE
- No ads, and no account to create. Purchases and subscriptions live in your Google Play account; we store nothing about you, and the store's proof is checked and forgotten.
- Buying, subscribing, settings, recording and backups sit behind a grown-ups gate.
- Optional anonymous usage counts with no names or identifiers, switched off in Settings.
- Privacy policy: https://dinoreader.sirous.uk/privacy.html
