# Dino Reader on LinkedIn: three storylines

Three films, built and shipped in the order the user set on 2026-11-20: **B** first (what Dino is, why it was built,
what it does), then **C** (how it was built), then **A** (the feature that was deleted). The user picked B for the first
film, so B is built here (`SPEC.md`); C and A start from this file in Slices 427 and 428.

Shared for all three: LinkedIn feed 4:5 at 1080 x 1350, 30 fps, 40-55 s, words on screen so it works muted, the app's own
voices (the pre-made Google Gemini clips of the onboarding page, Three Little Pigs page 13), real app screens, the
book's own pictures, free music, and the end card "Built in 5 weeks with Claude" with the three stores. Every claim
traces to the facts list of Slice 426 (`docs/plan/slices/426-dino-linkedin-film-b-bedtime.md`).

## B. One bedtime, three languages (picked; Slice 426)

- **Device and form:** a moment in someone's day (bedtime) carried by the feature as the hook; one frame that rebuilds:
  the same book page stays on screen while its words change language.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** one real page of The Three Little Pigs is read aloud by its own character voices
  in English, then Farsi, then German, the words on the page swapping script with the voice. The viewer gets, without a
  word of explanation, that one bedtime story can be heard in the language of each half of a family.
- **First two seconds:** page 13's picture (the three pigs at the brick fireplace with the bath tub) fills the frame,
  the English text card is already on it, and Pop's voice says "Uh-oh! He's going to come down the chimney!" while
  that line lights up yellow, as it does in the app.
- **Beats (as built, 44 s):** hook (English, with the caption card "A voice for every character" over 0-5.1 s) -> the
  same page in Farsi -> the same page in German -> "15 languages" -> why (children between two or three languages) ->
  record your voice (the real record screen, centred on its privacy line) -> "Built in 5 weeks with Claude" -> the three
  stores. The 3 s app recording was dropped (user, 2026-11-20).
- **Look:** the app's own: warm paper ground #F3EFE6, ink #2B2540, the Dino violet #5B57E8 as the one accent, the
  reading highlight yellow, Fredoka for titles, Andika for page text, Vazirmatn for Farsi; the book's painted pictures.
- **Sound:** the voices are the clock: the app's own clips for the page, then a soft bed under the why beat and the end
  card; short whooshes on the language swaps, one pop per store.

## C. Five weeks, fast-forward (Slice 427)

- **Device and form:** a number lands (5 weeks, the testing rounds counted up); one continuous zoom through the app's
  real screens week by week.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the app's real screens change week by week in one continuous zoom: old menus, old
  onboarding, then the new ones. A counter shows the testing rounds. The viewer feels the speed of the build.
- **First two seconds:** the oldest real Dino screen fills the frame with "Week 1" stamped over it, and the camera is
  already diving into one of its buttons.
- **Beats:** week 1 screen -> zoom through each week's screen with the round counter ticking -> today's app ->
  "Built in 5 weeks with Claude" -> the three stores.
- **Look:** the same Dino palette; the screens are the hero, each at full phone height; the counter in Fredoka.
- **Sound:** a steady pulse that speeds up with the weeks, one tick per round, a lift when today's app lands.

## A. The feature I deleted (Slice 428)

- **Device and form:** life before and after; object-to-object chain from the first idea to today's app.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** it opens on the first idea in the real early app: the scan screen and the
  built-in phone voice. The idea is struck out. Five quick "week" cards each show what testers asked for and the screen
  it led to. It ends on today's app: a book page, voices, languages and recording. The viewer feels the honesty of
  throwing work away for what users wanted.
- **First two seconds:** the real early scan screen with the robot phone voice reading a scanned page; a pen stroke
  starts to strike it out.
- **Beats:** first idea -> struck out -> five week cards (what testers asked, the screen it led to) -> today's app ->
  the three stores.
- **Look:** the Dino palette; the strike-out drawn as a hand-made marker stroke in the accent colour.
- **Sound:** the flat phone voice first, cut to silence on the strike, then the app's character voices with the bed.
