- **No phone voice (Slice 278 part B: docs/plan/slices/278-dino-store-books-only.md).** Nothing in Dino reads aloud with a speech engine, and nobody picks a voice: no cast screen, narrator page, voice preview, storyteller download or voice-pack download exists (`NoSpeechEngineTests` scans the sources and fails on `ISpeechReader`, `IVoicePackService`, `IVoiceModelService`, `VoicePackInstaller` and the like in `src/DinoReader.App`). A book plays its pre-made clips (`StoryVoicing` marks each piece with its clip) and the family's recordings; a piece with no clip and no recording is shown and highlighted for `PlaybackSteps.BeatMilliseconds`, reports one `premade_audio` fallback (`clip_missing`, or `clip_unplayable` when the file would not play) and the read goes on (`ReadingSession.BeginBeat`; Pause cancels the beat, Resume shows the piece again). `PlaybackSteps` (Core) is the pure plan. A saved book's `cast` / `languageCasts` are still read, only to find the same characters the pieces were cut at and the animals' sounds (`StoryVoicing.AnimalsOf`, over `CharacterRoster.Speakers`); nothing stores or edits one. The Mobile.Shared packages for the engine still reach the Dino packages, so the csproj keeps its three sherpa/onnxruntime imports (`AndroidSherpaPackaging.props`, `IosSherpaPackaging.props`, the WinML target): they neutralise those packages (wrong-OS natives, `APPX1101`, the iOS framework that Mobile.Shared's P/Invokes need). Drop them with the repo split. Debug builds only: the shared test hooks (`Mobile.Shared/Testing`) still resolve the speech reader.

Shelf language choices come only from `ShelfAudioPacks`' `InstalledShelfAudio` snapshot; no page reads pack folders. (Slice 278: docs/plan/slices/278-dino-store-books-only.md)
