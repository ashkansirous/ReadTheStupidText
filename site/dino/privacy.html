Privacy Policy — Dino Book Reader

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Dino Book Reader (“the app”) is a book reader for children aged 3 to 8. It reads stories aloud with a different voice for each character. This policy explains how the app handles data, for the children who use it and the grown-ups who set it up.

The short version

Dino Book Reader itself does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics of its own. Everything the app does happens on the device, apart from downloading voice packs and free stories. On Android, the Google ML Kit components the app uses send Google technical diagnostics data, described under “Google ML Kit (Android)” below. Your stories, photos, scans and audio are never sent.

What the app processes, and where

  • Stories you add (typed or pasted text, files, and photos or scans of book pages) are read and stored on the device only. They are never uploaded.
  • Photos and scans are turned into text by on-device text recognition. The picture stays on the device, in the app’s private storage, and is not sent to anyone.
  • Translation, where the app offers it, runs on the device with downloaded language models, or uses the translation that comes built into the story. Your text is never sent to a translation service.
  • Speech is generated on the device by an offline voice engine. No text or audio is sent to any server.
  • Settings and the story library (voices chosen for characters, reading progress, display choices) are stored on the device and never leave it.
  • The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not link children to other apps or websites.

Network use

Voice and language packs, and free stories. The app downloads voice and language packs (files it needs to speak, recognise and translate text) from this project’s public GitHub Releases page. The download is a plain file request: no account, no identifier, and none of the stories, photos or scans is sent. Once a pack is installed the app works offline.

Free stories. Behind the grown-ups gate, the app can show a store of free stories. Opening it fetches the list of stories, their cover pictures, and any story a grown-up chooses to get, from this project’s own server (dinoreader.sirous.uk). These are plain file requests: no account, no identifier, and nothing about the child, the device’s stories, photos or scans is sent. The app does not use cookies and does not record which stories were chosen.

Translation models (Android). When a story is read translated and the book has no built-in translation for that language, the phone downloads Google’s on-device translation model for the language, once. The story text is translated on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

Google ML Kit (Android). On Android the app uses Google’s ML Kit for the document scanner, text recognition and translation. ML Kit sends Google technical diagnostics and usage-analytics data over HTTPS: device information (manufacturer, model, operating system version), the app’s package name and version, performance metrics, API configuration, event types, error codes, the translation languages configured, and an identifier for the app installation that is not intended to identify a user or device (ML Kit also uses Firebase installations for remote configuration). ML Kit does not transfer this data to third parties. Story text, photos, scans and audio are never part of it. Google handles this data under Google’s Privacy Policy; see also Google’s ML Kit data disclosure. The Windows and iOS versions do not use ML Kit.

Like any web request, a download is seen by the server that hosts the file (GitHub for voice and language packs, Google for translation models on Android, this project’s own server for free stories), which may log the device’s IP address under its own privacy policy. Dino Book Reader itself does not receive or keep that information.

Permissions

The app declares the Internet and Network state permissions, used only to download voice and language packs, translation models and free stories, and to tell “offline” from a failed download. Pages you scan and photos you pick are processed on the device and never leave it. Choosing a file or photo uses the standard Android picker, and the app only opens what you pick.

Children’s privacy

Dino Book Reader is designed for children, and the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children: no name, email address, photos or stories. It has no accounts, no ads, no social features, no behavioural tracking, and no way for a child to send information to anyone. The only data that leaves the device is the technical diagnostics data that Google ML Kit sends on Android (see “Google ML Kit (Android)” above), which does not include any content a child creates. Settings and the story library sit behind a grown-ups gate. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct or delete beyond the content on their own device, which they can remove by deleting a story or clearing the app’s storage.
