Picture books that read themselves aloud, with a voice for every character.

Dino Book Reader is a picture-book reader for children aged 3 to 9. Pick stories from the free story store. Every character speaks in their own voice, and each sentence lights up as it is read. Free, no accounts, no ads.

A look inside

What it does

A voice for every character

Every character in a book has a pre-made voice. Families can record their own voice for each character, in each story language.

Three ways to read

Picture only, text, or picture and text. The sentence being read lights up. Reading speed goes from 0.8x to 1.2x, and animal sounds can be switched on or off.

A grown-ups area

Behind a grown-ups gate you find the Books, Store and Settings tabs. Back up your books and recordings, and restore them later.

A free story store

Books come from the story store, which is free to use. The store has 15 books.

Fifteen languages

The app, the books and the audio come in 15 languages, including Arabic and Farsi.

Works offline

Once a book and its language are downloaded, it reads without an internet connection.

Microsoft Store

Windows

Free on the Microsoft Store for Windows 10 and 11. It installs and updates like any other Store app.

Get it from the Microsoft Store

Free. No accounts, no ads. Once a book and its language are downloaded, it reads offline. The app sends only optional anonymous usage counts, with no names or identifiers, and a grown-up can switch them off in Settings.
