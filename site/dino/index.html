For ages 3–8

A storybook reader where every character has their own voice.

Scan a page, open a PDF or type a story, and Dino works out who is speaking and reads each character in their own voice, with a highlight that follows every sentence across the real page. No accounts, no ads; stories and photos never leave the device.

Android — Google Play (beta) iPhone and iPad — TestFlight (beta) Windows (coming to the Store) in testing

How it works

1

Add a story

Scan pages, import a PDF, or type or paste a story. Adding stories happens in the grown-up area: a hold, then a sum.

2

Dino casts the voices

A dialogue parser works out who is speaking and gives each character a voice. A grown-up picks the narrator, and a cast screen lets you change voices.

3

Read along

Pick picture only, picture and text, or text only. The highlight follows each sentence; swipe or tap next to turn the page.

What it does

A voice for every character

Each line is attributed to a speaker, and the voice caster keeps that voice for the whole book. Unknown speakers get the narrator.

Picture only, picture and text, or text only

The spoken sentence is highlighted on the real scanned page or PDF, with the words shown on it, or in a clean text view. Pages turn with a swipe or the next button.

Story gallery and grown-up area

Add stories by typing, pasting, opening a file, scanning pages or importing a PDF, and edit or delete them.

A cast screen

Choose each character’s voice and preview it.

Nine languages

With right-to-left layout for Farsi and Arabic, and the same on-device neural voices and OCR as Read The Stupid Text.

Works offline

After the voice pack download it works offline. It only sends small anonymous counts (no names, nothing a child types), which a grown-up can switch off.
