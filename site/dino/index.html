For ages 3–8

Dino Reader

A book reader where every character has their own voice. Scan a page or open a story, and Dino reads it aloud, highlighting each sentence as it goes.

  • A voice for every characterDino works out who is speaking and keeps each character’s voice for the whole book. A grown-up picks the narrator.
  • A highlight on every pageFollow along with the spoken sentence, in the real scanned page or in a clean text view.
  • Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no tracking. Stories and photos never leave the device, and reading works offline.
