Reads any text aloud in a natural voice, offline, at your speed.

Select or copy text, press Ctrl+Win+R, or open a file, and it reads it aloud in a natural on-device neural voice at any speed from 0.5x to 2x. A Windows tray app on the Microsoft Store, with Android and iPhone apps that read a page straight from the camera. Nothing you read leaves the device.

Windows — Microsoft Store Android beta — Google Play (2 steps) iPhone and iPad beta — TestFlight (2 steps)

Android and iPhone/iPad are in beta. iPhone and iPad: anyone can join; first install Apple’s TestFlight app, then come back to this page and tap step 2.

How text gets in

🖱️

Auto-read on selection

Select text in any app and it starts reading.

📋

Auto-read on copy

Copy text, including from the terminal, and it reads it aloud.

⌨️

Global hotkey

Press Ctrl + Win + R from any app, including the terminal.

📷

On your phone

Point the camera at a page or use the multi-page document scanner.

What it does

🔊

Natural voices on the device

Natural voices in fifteen languages, including ten English voices such as “Momonga”. Voice packs download once (about 145 MB); the built-in system voice reads until the pack arrives. Fully offline afterwards.

⏩

Speed 0.5x to 2x

Change it live mid-read, with presets. Skip 10 seconds back or forward, see a read-through timer, and swap voice while the current read continues.

📄

Reads your files

Open a .txt, .pdf or .docx file and have it read aloud.

🎛️

Tray control panel

Waveform, progress, speed pill, voice and language pickers, a draggable window, and an activity log that explains every read.

🌐

Translate to my language

On-device machine translation turns selected, copied, scanned or opened text into your language before it is read (Windows and Android). The text language is detected automatically.

⚡

Head start

The first sentences are prepared while you look at the text, and heard sentences are cached, so reading starts almost instantly.

🔒

Private

No account. Text, files and scans never leave the device. The app only sends small anonymous counts (no names, nothing you read), which you can switch off.

📷

Camera and scanner

Text is recognised on the phone, with ML Kit and downloaded PaddleOCR models for Arabic, Farsi, Russian, Chinese and Japanese.
