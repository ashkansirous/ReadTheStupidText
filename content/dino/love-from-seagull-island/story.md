One sunny morning, Pancake Polly fished a bottle out of the sea with her big net. Inside was a letter, and a picture of two smiling seagulls!

"Seagull! This letter is for you!" said Pancake Polly. "It says: Dear Seagull, we're coming to visit! Lots of love, Granny Seagull and Grampy Seagull."
---
Seagull held the picture in her wings. There were Granny and Grampy, smiling right at her!

"Squawk! Squawk! Squawk! I love them so, so much!" said Seagull.

Then she went a little bit quiet. "But we haven't played together for a long, long time. What will I do with them?" said Seagull.
---
"Granny and Grampy Seagull are coming? Hooray!" said Kathryn.

Kathryn and Pancake Polly gave Seagull a great big hug, and they all danced round and round the deck.

"Don't worry, Seagull. Give them a big hug, and you'll know just what to play!" said Kathryn.
---
A few days later, splash! A little plane landed beside the ship. Out hopped Granny Seagull and Grampy Seagull. Seagull Island was much too far for wings!

They gave Seagull a great big hug. "We love you more than the whole wide world!" said Granny Seagull.

"I missed you so much!" said Seagull, and she snuggled right in between them.
---
Seagull didn't want to leave Granny and Grampy's side, not even for a minute. She sat with them at breakfast. She sat with them at lunch.

"You're our little shadow!" said Grampy Seagull, and he gave her a feathery tickle.
---
And now Seagull knew just what to play! "I'm a busy bee! Bzzz, bzzz, bzzz!" said Seagull.

She buzzed round and round Grampy Seagull and gave him a tickly pretend sting. It was as soft as a feather. It tickled!

"Ooh! Help, help! I'm too hot! I need a firefighter!" giggled Grampy Seagull. It was all just pretend, of course.
---
Granny Seagull put on a big red helmet and grabbed the ship's hose. "Don't worry, Grampy! Firefighter Granny is here!" said Granny Seagull.

Psssh! She sprinkled cool water all over him.

"Ahh, that's much better!" said Grampy Seagull. And everybody laughed and laughed.
---
Next, Grampy Seagull hid under a big, soft blanket. "Oh no! I'm stuck! Who can get me out?" said Grampy Seagull, wiggling and giggling.

Seagull held her wing up to her ear like a phone. "Nee-naw, nee-naw! Police! Police! Come quick! Grampy is stuck!" said Seagull.
---
Granny Seagull put on a blue police cap and came running. "Police Granny to the rescue!" said Granny Seagull.

One, two, three, whoosh! She pulled off the blanket. And there was Grampy Seagull, laughing and laughing.

"Thank you, Police Granny!" said Grampy Seagull.
---
That evening, Granny Seagull made her special Seagull Island sea-berry cake. Only seagulls know how to make it!

Seagull took a big bite. "Yum, yum, yum! It's the yummiest cake ever!" said Seagull.

"Ooh, it's even yummier than my pancakes!" said Pancake Polly.
---
The days went by, full of games and hugs. One evening, Granny Seagull said it was time to fly home. Their cosy home on Seagull Island was waiting.

Seagull's head drooped. She would miss Granny and Grampy so much, and Grandma and Grandpa too, her other grandparents far away.

"We'll miss you too, little one," said Grampy Seagull.
---
Kathryn and Pancake Polly hugged Seagull gently.

"When Granny and Grampy fly home, you can still see them on my phone," said Kathryn. "Grannies and grandpas love their grandchildren so much. They want to see them every chance they get, for a visit or on a video call! And you'll see them again!"

Seagull snuggled in and felt much better.
---
The next morning, the little plane was waiting on the water. Granny Seagull and Grampy Seagull kissed Seagull goodbye. Mwah! Mwah!

"Bye-bye, little Seagull! We'll call you on the phone, and we'll see you again!" said Grampy Seagull.

"Bye-bye, Granny! Bye-bye, Grampy! I love you!" said Seagull.

Then they hopped on the plane.
---
Brrrm! The little plane flew up, up, up, over the sea, all the way home to Seagull Island.

Seagull, Kathryn and Pancake Polly waved and waved, until the plane was just a tiny dot in the sky.
---
That evening, Kathryn's phone rang. Ring, ring! On the screen were Granny and Grampy, home on Seagull Island, and Grandma and Grandpa too!

"We all miss you, and we love you more than the whole wide world!" they said together.

"I love you more than the whole wide world too!" said Seagull. She blew them air kisses. Mwah! Mwah! Mwah!
---
That night, Seagull snuggled down on Kathryn's big hat and looked up at the stars.

She still missed Granny and Grampy, and Grandma and Grandpa too. But she knew they loved her, near or far. And she couldn't wait to see them all again!

"Squawk, squawk, squawk," said Seagull softly, and she closed her eyes.
