# Good Enough: art record

Bundled starter (`StarterBuilder.GoodEnoughId`, stem `good-enough`, file `good-enough.dino`, version 4), ages 5-8, author Ashkan Sirous.
The user's own picture book (Slice 94), built from its pictures and `content/dino/good-enough/lines.json`, not from a `story.md`.
An original story, not a retelling. Present tense throughout (the printed pages are present tense; pages 8-10 match).

**10 pages, all pictures kept.** Pages 1-7 have the English words **printed in the picture**, with a box per printed line in
`lines.json` (`x`/`y`/`w`/`h`) for the highlight. Their English must match the printed words exactly, so it is never reworded
unless the pictures are redrawn: Slice 146 Wave B (spoken English, 2026-10-27) reworded only pages 8-10, which have no printed
words (`"text"`, no `lines`). The translations of every page were redone in the spoken register (Decision 152).

**Gentle story (Decision 130):** nobody is hurt or told off. Carrot runs onto the pitch out of excitement; the players only stop
and stare; the referee walks them back and Carrot says sorry; Hazelnut closes with the point of the book. Calm ending, together.

**Sound words (Decision 151):** `Zoom, zoom, zoom!` (page 8), one piece so each language writes its own triple. Measured
languages: en, es, zh, fa. Not yet listened to in ar, da, fi, fr, ja, nb, nl, pt, ru, sv (flag for a listen).

## House style

Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolour and coloured pencil, warm gentle colours, simple rounded shapes, no text
or letters anywhere. (The existing pictures predate this line: they carry the printed story words on pages 1-7 and signs with
"Bright Stadium", "Great Views, Row C" and "Exit". A redraw would need the words printed again or the lines removed.)

## Characters

- **Carrot**: a lop-eared rabbit, tan with a white tummy, the taller of the two; blue-and-white striped scarf; no clothes, no prop.
  Never speaks (no voice). Told apart from Hazelnut by the long floppy ears and the blue scarf.
- **Hazelnut**: a grey-brown squirrel, a head shorter than Carrot, big bushy tail; orange-and-white striped scarf; no clothes.
  Speaks once (page 10). Told apart by the bushy tail and the orange scarf.
- **The referee**: a badger, much taller than both, black-and-white striped referee shirt, white shorts, striped socks, black
  boots; a whistle. Does not speak.
- **The players**: cats in orange and light-blue shirts with numbers (10, 18, 26). Background only.
- **The crowd**: people and a few animals in scarves and team shirts. Background only.

## Voices (Slice 156, `StarterVoices`)

- **Hazelnut**: suggested roster voice `bee` (Busy Bee, "bubbly and quick"), female, young, no person kind (a squirrel has no
  animal sound, so no kind is stated), qualities Bright, Warm. Female as the shipped cast already had her (supertonic F1) and as
  Slice 94's test calls her; the Arabic translation now uses her feminine verbs (تقترح، تقول). Where `bee` is missing or sits on
  the narrator's voice (Farsi, mana) the app matches the traits.
- Carrot, the referee and the players have no lines, so no cast entry.

## Picture plan (one line per page; all ten existing pictures keep)

1. Carrot and Hazelnut stand at the stadium gate, excited; 2 friends plus small fans behind; outside the arch; day. Keep.
2. They sit in the back row and can't see the ball; 2 friends, crowd; top of the stands; day. Keep.
3. Carrot wishes to see better while Hazelnut points down; 2 friends; back rows; day. Keep.
4. They wiggle through tall legs down a few rows; 2 friends, crowd legs; the stands' stairs; day. Keep.
5. They have a good view and Carrot leans forward wanting closer; 2 friends; middle rows; day. Keep.
6. They sneak closer down the rows; 2 friends; lower rows; day. Keep.
7. They reach the front row and Carrot keeps going; 2 friends; front row by the barrier; day. Keep.
8. Carrot runs past the front row onto the grass; 2 friends, crowd behind; edge of the pitch by the Row C sign; day. Keep.
9. The players all stop and stare at the two in the middle of the field; 2 friends, about 20 cat players; centre circle; late afternoon light. Keep.
10. The referee walks them back to Row C and Carrot says sorry; 2 friends, 1 referee, crowd; by the Row C sign; day. Keep.
