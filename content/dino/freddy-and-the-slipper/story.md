Freddy the fox was a beautiful fox,
not tall like a tree and not small like a box.
He had twinkly eyes and a warm, happy smile,
and a fluffy red tail that went swish all the while.
"I'm clever, I'm kind, and I'm wise," Freddy said,
"and I love my soft tail and my fluffy red head!"
---
One evening his Mummy called, "Dinner, my dear!
I've made you some stew. Come and sit over here!"
But Freddy just nibbled a carrot or two,
a cucumber slice, and a crust he could chew.
---
"Just one little spoonful," said Mummy. "It's yummy!
It's chicken and peas!" Said Freddy, "No room in my tummy!"
"Psst! Freddy! I'm empty!" Tummy said.
But Freddy just shook his fluffy red head.
---
"Night-night, my Freddy," said Mummy. "Sleep tight!"
"Night-night," said Freddy, and switched off the light.
---
But there in the dark, from under the sheet,
came a rumble-grumble! Tummy cried, "Freddy! I want to eat!"
"Shh, Tummy!" said Freddy. "Go to sleep, please!"
"Chicken! A burger! Kebabs!" Tummy squeaked. "Feed me! Feed me, please!"
---
So Freddy crept down to the kitchen to look.
He searched every cupboard, each corner and nook.
He peeked in the fridge and he peeked in the pot.
"Anything tasty?" asked Tummy. Said Freddy, "Er... not."
---
"I know!" whispered Freddy. "Out there on the grill,
Daddy made kebabs! Maybe one's there still!"
"Kebab!" cheered Tummy. "Hooray! Yum-yum-yum!
Go, Freddy, go! Get a kebab for your tum!"
---
So out to the garden went the fox with a hop,
and he tiptoed up close to the grill. Then... stop!
He looked at the cold grill with hope in his eyes.
"Hurry up!" grumbled Tummy with rumbly cries.
---
"Look!" Freddy squeaked. "Something brown! Under the grill!"
And he jumped up and down with joy and a thrill!
---
He skipped to it, grabbed it, and gave it a chomp!
Chew, chew! Munch, munch! It tasted like swamp!
"Ewww! Yuck!" yelled Freddy, and spat it out. "What kebab is this meat?
It's chewy and rubbery... and smells like old feet!"
"Bleurgh!" cried Tummy. "Don't send that down here!
That's not a kebab! What is that? Oh dear!"
---
"It's not a kebab! It's a smelly old shoe!
Is it a slipper? A sandal? I don't have a clue!"
"A clog?" asked Tummy. "A flip-flop?" Said Freddy, "Oh! Wait! Now I know!
It's Daddy's old slipper! Yuck! Oh no, oh no, no!"
---
Poor Freddy was hungry and grumpy and sad.
"Rumble-grumble," moaned Tummy. "Oh, this is so bad!"
---
So Freddy crept back inside, and what did he find?
Some cold, plain potatoes, the not-yummy kind.
"Cold, plain potatoes?" grumbled Tummy. "Boo-hoo!"
"I know," sighed Freddy, "but they'll have to do."
He munched them up slowly. Then, with a frown,
"That's what you get when you turn dinner down!"
---
The very next night, Mummy said, "Stew, my dear!"
"Yes, please!" said Freddy. "I'm sitting right here!"
He ate every bite, then he asked for some more.
"Yum-yum!" sang Tummy. "That's what tummies are for!"
